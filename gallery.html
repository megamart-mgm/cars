Driver Story

Lost Wallet, Returned In Full

During a trip in Lagos, one of our drivers found a wallet left behind by a passenger — cash, cards, and all. Rather than keep it, he tracked the passenger down and handed it back untouched.

The passenger, visibly moved by the gesture, rewarded him with $200 on the spot — and the story went on to be picked up and shared as a feel-good moment online.

It's a small moment, but it says a lot about the people behind DOSB'O Synergy: the same integrity we bring to a build site, a shipment, or a leased apartment is the same integrity our drivers carry on the road.
