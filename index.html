About DOSB'O SYNERGY LIMITED

DOSB'O Synergy Limited (RN: 6992198) is a Lagos-based multi-sector company bringing together General Contracting, Logistics, Import & Export, Vehicle Hire & Transportation, and Apartment Leasing & Airbnb management under one dependable name.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before working with us.

Do you handle both local and international logistics? expand_more
Yes. We manage local haulage and delivery across Nigeria, as well as international freight tied to our import and export services.
What documentation do you provide for import/export orders? expand_more
We prepare and support customs-ready documentation so your goods move without unnecessary delays.
Can I hire a vehicle with a driver, or self-drive only? expand_more
Both. Most bookings come with a chauffeur, but self-drive hire is available for qualifying clients on request.
How do you manage Airbnb and leased apartments? expand_more
Every unit is personally inspected and maintained, with hands-on handling of guest bookings, tenant needs, and upkeep.
